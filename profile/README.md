# TacApp

**Freedom Software for the 2A industry.**

TacApp is a white-label app platform for independent gun shops, shooting ranges
and training companies: loyalty, class booking, digital waivers, check-in and
direct customer messaging — under the store's own brand.

- No 4473 data, no serial numbers, no receipt line items. Data we never collect can't leak.
- Works alongside any point-of-sale system.
- Your customers, your channel. No middleman between the store and its people.
